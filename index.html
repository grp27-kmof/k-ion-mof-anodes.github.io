Computational Design and Generation of High-Performance MOF Anodes for K-Ion Batteries

Executive summary

Potassium-ion batteries are a low-cost alternative to lithium-ion, but potassium’s large ionic radius rules out anodes like graphite. MOFs have tunable pores, yet millions of metal–linker combinations cannot be tested by hand. We built a validation pipeline and two generative models, MOFDiff and EGMOF, to find MOF anodes for K-ion batteries. So far, three generated batches have been validated, and one EGMOF candidate has passed every stage, with DFT running.

ALIGNNgraph neural network
  • Builds a graph from atomic species, bond distances and coordination.
  • Predicts normalised formation energy.
  • Keeps MOFs with Eform < 0.
CHGNetuniversal MLIP · binding and dynamics
  • Input: about 4,000 ALIGNN-filtered structures (QMOF subset plus generated MOFs), after checking that K⁺ can be inserted.
  • K-site search: tries several sites, relaxes only the K ion, and takes the global minimum Eads.
  • Staged MD at 300 K: 10 ps NVT (Berendsen), keep top 20%, then 50 ps NVT (Nosé–Hoover).
  • Ranking weights: adsorption 40%, MSI 35%, MSD 20%, confidence 5%.
  • Top candidates go to MACE.
MACEequivariant MLIP · diffusion
  • CIFCleaner removes residual disorder and solvent artefacts.
  • BVSE or DistinctPathFinder finds the hop path; K is snapped onto MACE-relaxed sites.
  • 7-image CI-NEB with FIRE gives the barrier Ea.
  • D = D0 exp(−Ea/kBT) and σ = nz²e²D/kBT.
  • Bounds: Ea ≤ 0.5 eV and σ ≥ 10⁻⁴ S/cm. Survivors go to DFT.
CP2K DFTfirst-principles check
  • Quickstep (GPW) with a 400 Ry plane-wave cutoff and DZVP-MOLOPT basis sets.
  • Solver routing: OT + CG for gapped systems; Broyden + Fermi smearing for metallic or mixed-valence ones.
  • DFT+U on 3d metals (4.0 eV Fe, 6.4 eV Ni); switched off for 4d/5d dichalcogenides such as MoS₂.
  • CI-NEB on pre-relaxed endpoints for the K⁺ barrier.
  • Outputs: Ehull, Eads, OCV, ΔV, DOS, Bader and Hirshfeld charges.

Pipeline test on three different archetypes.
MOFDiffcoarse-grained diffusion
  • Each node and linker becomes one bead in a coarse graph, stored in LMDB.
  • A building-block encoder is trained, then a diffusion model on its embeddings.
  • Generation starts from noise, refined step by step into a bead arrangement.
  • Beads are rebuilt into atoms and output as a CIF, then relaxed and measured before Phase 1.
EGMOFdiffusion + transformer · property-conditioned
  • Two stages: a 1D diffusion model (Prop2Desc) turns target properties into descriptors.
  • A transformer (Desc2MOF) generates the MOF structure from those descriptors.
  • Stages are modular, so new target properties need little or no retraining.
  • Being retrained on our curated dataset. Outputs go through the same Phase 1 filters.
